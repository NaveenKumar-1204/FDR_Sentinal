The idea in 30 seconds
What is an FDR?
A Fixed Deposit Receipt. Colleges put money in a bank and give AICTE the FDR as a security deposit.
What goes wrong?
Fake FDRs, the same FDR shown by two colleges, or the college quietly closing the deposit early.
What this app does
Reads the FDR, asks the bank if it is real, has the bank lock it for AICTE, and gives a QR receipt anyone can check.

The main journey

Every FDR goes through these four steps, in this order. Each FDR's page shows a progress bar with the same steps.

  1. Add the FDR

    Upload a scan or phone photo. The app reads the details.

  2. Bank confirms

    The bank says the FDR is genuine.

  3. Bank locks it

    The deposit is locked for AICTE (a "lien"), so nobody can close it early.

  4. Give QR receipt

    A signed receipt that anyone can scan to check.

Screen catalogue

Grouped the way they appear in the left menu. "More tools" is a fold-out at the bottom of the menu.

Main menu

Home

Menu → Home

Your start page. Shows how many FDRs need you and your to-do list.

  1. Look at the big headline: how many FDRs need attention.
  2. Click any item in Your to-do list to fix it.
  3. Open More numbers for charts.
Open Home →

Add an FDR

Menu → Add an FDR

Upload an FDR. The app reads the number, bank, amount and dates for you.

  1. Upload a photo, scan or PDF, or pick a sample under No FDR to hand? Try a sample.
  2. Wait a few seconds while it reads.
  3. Green fields are sure. Yellow ones: compare with the scan, fix, then tick.
  4. Click Save FDR.
Open Add an FDR →

Needs attention

Menu → Needs attention

Every problem found, most urgent first. Each one tells you what to do.

  1. Use the chips at the top to show only urgent ones.
  2. Read the What to do line.
  3. Click Open FDR to act on it.
Open Needs attention →

All FDRs

Menu → All FDRs

The full list of FDRs. Problems are listed first.

  1. Search by FDR number, college or bank.
  2. Filter: Needs action, Waiting for bank or Safe.
  3. Click a row to open that FDR's page.
Open All FDRs →

An FDR's page

All FDRs → click any FDR

Everything about one deposit, and the one button you should press next. This is where most of the work happens.

  1. Look at the progress bar: Added → Bank confirmed → Locked for AICTE → QR receipt given.
  2. Press the button next to it. It changes with the step: Ask to confirm, then Ask to lock, then Give QR receipt, then View receipt.
  3. Check Problems found and compare the uploaded scan with the details.
  4. Record your call: Looks genuine or Investigate.
  5. For audits, open Advanced details: similar FDRs, history, risk score and timeline.

What the status labels mean

You will see these coloured labels on every list.

SafeBank confirmed it and locked it. Nothing to do.
Safe · matures soonSafe, but it ends soon. Remind the college to renew.
Waiting for bankYou asked the bank. No reply yet.
Needs actionA problem was found, such as a duplicate. Open it and follow "What to do".
Bank problemThe bank said the details differ, or it has no such deposit.
Closed at bankThe deposit was closed. AICTE is no longer protected.
ReturnedGiven back to the college. Finished.

Good to know

  • All data is made up. No real colleges, banks or deposits.
  • Each browser has its own copy. What you add on your laptop does not appear on someone else's phone.
  • Bank view syncs only with tabs in the same browser. Open it as a new tab, not on another device.
  • Want to start over? Settings → Clear local data.
  • It is a prototype. The bank is simulated and the receipt key is a demo key.
