FDR Sentinel X — Quick Guide

How to use the dashboard. Open it at fdr-sentinel-x.vercel.app

What is an FDR?A Fixed Deposit Receipt. Colleges put money in a bank and give AICTE the FDR as a security deposit. What goes wrong?Fake FDRs, the same FDR shown by two colleges, or the deposit closed early in secret. What the app doesReads the FDR, asks the bank if it is real, has the bank lock it for AICTE, and gives a QR receipt anyone can check.

The 4 steps every FDR goes through

1Add the FDR

Upload a scan or photo. The app reads it.

2Bank confirms

The bank says it is genuine.

3Bank locks it

Locked for AICTE, so it can't be closed early.

4Give QR receipt

A signed receipt anyone can scan.

Good to know

  • All data is made up. No real colleges, banks or deposits.
  • Each browser keeps its own copy. What you add on your laptop won't show on another device.
  • Bank view syncs only with tabs in the same browser.
  • Start over: Settings → Clear local data.
  • Prototype: the bank is simulated and the receipt key is a demo key.
